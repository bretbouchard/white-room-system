# Song

Your composition. Theory and craft combined.

---

## What This Is

The Song pillar encompasses everything related to musical composition:

- **Theory** — The mathematical foundation (Schillinger System)
- **Songwriting** — The creative application of theory

---

## Layers

### [Theory](./theory/) — Schillinger System

The mathematical foundation for composition:

- Rhythm resultants and interference patterns
- Melodic contour and motivic development
- Harmonic progressions and voice leading
- Orchestration and form

### [Songwriting](./songwriting/) — Creative Application

The craft of turning theory into songs:

- Song structure and arrangement
- Hook construction
- Emotional arc design

---

## Relationship

```
THEORY                          SONGWRITING
────────                        ───────────
Schillinger System      →       Creative choices
Mathematical rules      →       Artistic judgment
Form, Rhythm, Melody    →       Hooks, Arc, Structure
```

Theory provides the foundation. Songwriting makes it musical.
